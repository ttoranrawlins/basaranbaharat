Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum wholesale order?

The minimum depends on the product and packaging. Tell us what you need and roughly how much, and we will work it out together.

Do you publish a price list?

Spice prices change with season, product and quantity, so we do not publish fixed prices online. Call us or use the quote tool for current prices.

Which products do you carry?

Cumin, sumac, mint, thyme/oregano (kekik) and bay leaf. If you need something else, call and ask.

Can I visit the shop?

Yes. Our shop is in Konak Food Bazaar at Yenişehir Mah. 1202/11 Sk. No:7. We recommend calling before you come.

Are samples and packaging options available?

Call us to talk about samples and packaging. We will see what is possible for the product and quantity.
